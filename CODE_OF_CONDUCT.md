# Code of conduct

NaiveN should be a place where contributors can do useful work without dealing with harassment, personal attacks, or needless hostility.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct and respectful.
- Critique code and design decisions, not people.
- Assume contributors have different levels of time, context, and experience.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Give clear reasons when you disagree.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal insults.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Doxing or sharing private information.
- Repeated disruption of issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Dismissing people based on identity, background, or experience level.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block participants who violate this code of conduct.

If you need to report a problem, contact the maintainers through the project's security or maintainer contact channel. Include links, screenshots, and context when possible. Reports will be handled with care and shared only with people who need the information to respond.
